Garlic Bread Recipe

Welcome to my recipe! Hope you like it!

Step 1 Step 2 Step 3

Step 1 - The Bread

Bake it in the oven at 400ºF like sourdough bread.

Step 2 - The Garlic

Ferment some garlic cloves in olive oil (confit), then smash and spread over toasted bread from step 1.

Step 3 - Enjoy!

Eat it and enjoy your creation!
